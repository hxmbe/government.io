Practice Questions

1. Which best explains consistently higher turnout among non-Hispanic white citizens compared to Hispanic and Asian American citizens?

(A) Hispanic and Asian American citizens are constitutionally barred from voting in primary elections in most states.
(B) Structural factors such as lower registration rates, language barriers, less targeted mobilization, and higher residential mobility contribute to lower turnout.
(C) Federal law requires voter registration forms be printed only in English, suppressing turnout among non-English speakers.
(D) Hispanic and Asian American voters prefer voting by mail, and their ballots are more frequently rejected on technical grounds.

2. The decline in voter turnout across all groups from 2020 to 2024 is most likely explained by which of the following?

(A) Congress restricted early voting and vote-by-mail options between 2020 and 2024, depressing national turnout.
(B) The 2020 election featured uniquely high mobilization due to COVID-19 mail voting expansion and heightened political salience, while 2024 returned closer to historical norms.
(C) Removal of Donald Trump from the ballot reduced Republican enthusiasm, causing turnout to fall across all groups.
(D) Electoral College reform between 2020 and 2024 reduced the perceived stakes of the presidential election.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → B
