Practice Questions

1. The trend most directly illustrates which concept in American politics?

(A) Dealignment, in which voters abandon both major parties for independent identification.
(B) Ideological sorting, in which both parties have become more internally homogeneous as moderates exit or are marginalized.
(C) Realignment, in which large blocs of voters switch parties in a single dramatic election.
(D) Third-party growth, in which new parties emerge to fill the vacuum left by party moderates.

2. Which consequence would a political scientist most likely predict for the legislative process?

(A) Ideological homogeneity within each party will make bipartisan compromise easier since each can negotiate as a unified bloc.
(B) As ideological overlap between the parties shrinks, finding common ground for bipartisan legislation becomes more difficult, increasing the likelihood of legislative gridlock.
(C) More ideologically consistent parties will produce more moderate candidates in primary elections.
(D) Consistent ideological identity within each party strengthens third-party alternatives by creating a clear target for criticism.
