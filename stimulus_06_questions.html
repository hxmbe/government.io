Practice Questions

1. Which best explains why FDR's veto total dramatically exceeds all others?

(A) FDR served longer than any other president and Congress passed a far greater volume of legislation during his four terms.
(B) FDR was constitutionally required to veto legislation conflicting with executive branch regulations.
(C) Congressional gridlock during World War II forced FDR to veto most domestic legislation.
(D) FDR's Democratic majority sent him opposition bills to demonstrate congressional independence.

2. The relatively low veto counts for recent presidents most likely reflect which of the following?

(A) Recent presidents lacked the political courage to oppose Congress.
(B) Modern presidents increasingly rely on executive orders and other unilateral tools rather than the veto to shape policy outcomes.
(C) The Supreme Court has recently limited the president's veto power.
(D) Congress has passed significantly less legislation, giving presidents fewer bills to consider.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → A    Q2 → B
