Practice Questions

1. Near-zero Super PAC spending before 2010 reflects which legal reality?

(A) The FEC prohibited all outside spending in federal elections until a rule change in 2009.
(B) Super PACs did not legally exist before 2010; they were created as a direct result of Citizens United v. FEC (2010) and the subsequent SpeechNow.org v. FEC decision.
(C) Congress passed the Bipartisan Campaign Reform Act in 2002 permanently banning all outside spending until 2010.
(D) Political parties lobbied to keep all campaign funds within their organizations until the Court intervened.

2. Based on the data, which argument about Super PACs and democratic accountability is best supported?

(A) Super PAC spending strengthens democratic accountability by providing voters with more information about candidates.
(B) The rapid growth of Super PAC spending raises accountability concerns, since these groups can accept unlimited contributions without the same transparency requirements as official campaigns.
(C) Super PAC spending has effectively replaced all candidate-controlled campaign spending, making candidates less powerful than outside groups.
(D) The data shows Super PAC spending is concentrated in midterm elections where voter turnout is typically higher.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → B
