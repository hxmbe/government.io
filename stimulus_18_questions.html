Practice Questions

1. The dramatic increase in campaign spending after 2010 is most directly connected to which Supreme Court decision?

(A) McCulloch v. Maryland (1819), which established the federal government could regulate interstate commerce including campaign finance.
(B) Citizens United v. FEC (2010), which held that political spending by corporations, associations, and unions is a form of protected free speech, removing limits on independent expenditures.
(C) Marbury v. Madison (1803), which established judicial review and gave the Court authority to strike down campaign finance restrictions.
(D) Buckley v. Valeo (1976), which first established the constitutional link between money and speech in campaign finance.

2. Critics of the trend would most likely argue rising campaign spending threatens democratic principles by doing which of the following?

(A) Creating an uneven playing field in which candidates with access to large donors and outside spending groups gain structural advantages over those without.
(B) Causing voter fatigue and reducing overall turnout by flooding voters with too many advertisements.
(C) Violating the equal protection clause by giving wealthy donors more First Amendment rights than ordinary citizens.
(D) Forcing incumbents to raise more money than challengers, making it harder for new candidates to compete.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → A
