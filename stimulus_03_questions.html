Practice Questions

1. Which best explains why the Bill of Rights era produced more amendments than any other period?

(A) The original Constitution was too vague about presidential powers and required clarification.
(B) Anti-Federalists demanded explicit individual rights protections as a condition of ratifying the Constitution, leading to the first ten amendments.
(C) The Supreme Court ruled the Constitution was unconstitutional without a written list of rights.
(D) Congress added the Bill of Rights to prevent states from seceding from the new union.

2. The cluster of amendments during the Progressive Era most directly reflects which political development?

(A) Southern states demanded constitutional protections for states rights following Reconstruction.
(B) Reformers used the amendment process to expand democracy and government power, enacting the income tax, direct election of senators, Prohibition, and women's suffrage.
(C) The Supreme Court struck down major federal legislation, prompting Congress to override with amendments.
(D) Third-party movements pressured both parties to pass structural electoral reforms.
