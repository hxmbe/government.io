## Q47 - Topic 5.11 - Free Response: Policy Brief Prompt

mobile young voters who frequently change addresses.

creating administrative errors and potentially inflating voter rolls - raising election integrity concerns.

*Scoring Guide:*
- Thesis: Clear, defensible claim (1 pt)

## Q55 - Topic Synthesis - AP Exam Free Response Practice

**Concept Application Prompt:**

The United States has experienced significant political polarization in recent decades.

**Part A:** Describe ONE cause of increased political polarization in the United States.

*Model Answer:* Geographic sorting - Americans have increasingly moved into politically homogeneous
communities, reducing cross-partisan contact and creating media environments that reinforce existing
beliefs. This reduces the shared experiences that once produced moderate political views.

**Part B:** Explain how political polarization affects ONE institution of the federal government.

*Model Answer:* Congress - polarization has increased party-line voting, empowered ideologically
extreme factions in both parties, made bipartisan legislation harder to pass, and incentivized
members to prioritize base mobilization over compromise. The result is legislative gridlock even
on issues with broad public support.

**Part C:** Describe ONE way that political parties have responded to the rise of polarization.

*Model Answer:* Parties have intensified base mobilization strategies over persuasion of swing voters,
investing in micro-targeting, small-dollar fundraising from engaged partisans, and ideologically
consistent messaging that activates negative partisanship rather than building broad coalitions.
