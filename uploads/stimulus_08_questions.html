Practice Questions

1. The decline in federal civilian employment in the mid-1990s is most directly associated with which of the following?

(A) The Supreme Court ruled that the size of the bureaucracy was unconstitutional under separation of powers.
(B) The Clinton administration's reinventing government initiative and budget agreements with the Republican Congress reduced the federal workforce.
(C) Congress passed a constitutional amendment limiting federal employees to fewer than 3 million.
(D) A series of recessions forced the federal government to lay off large numbers of civilian workers.

2. The relatively stable size of the federal workforce supports which conclusion?

(A) The bureaucracy has been largely ineffective because its size has not kept pace with U.S. population growth.
(B) Overall employee numbers have remained stable even as spending and regulatory activity grew, partly because of increased outsourcing to private contractors.
(C) Presidential ideology has no meaningful effect on the size of the federal civilian workforce.
(D) The bureaucracy shrinks significantly under Republicans and grows under Democrats, creating a clear partisan pattern.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → B
