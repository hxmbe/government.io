Practice Questions

1. Which best explains why freedom of religion and speech receive higher support than the right to petition?

(A) The Supreme Court has expanded speech and religious protections far beyond original intent, making them more visible.
(B) Freedom of religion and speech are more directly experienced in daily life and more prominent in political discourse, making them more salient to survey respondents.
(C) The right to petition was added later and is therefore less constitutionally protected than other First Amendment rights.
(D) Polling consistently shows Americans prioritize civil liberties over political participation rights.

2. A civil libertarian reviewing this data would most likely argue which of the following?

(A) Because all five rights receive majority support, there is no risk Congress will curtail First Amendment protections.
(B) The gap between support for speech and religion versus assembly and petition suggests some First Amendment rights are more politically vulnerable to restriction.
(C) The data confirms that the incorporation doctrine has successfully educated the public about constitutional rights.
(D) High support levels demonstrate that linkage institutions have successfully communicated constitutional values.
