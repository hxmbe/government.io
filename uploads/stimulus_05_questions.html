Practice Questions

1. The dramatic increase in cloture motions after the 112th Congress most directly reflects which trend?

(A) A constitutional amendment expanded Senate power to review executive nominations.
(B) Increased partisan polarization led the minority party to use the filibuster more frequently to block majority legislation.
(C) The Senate formally changed its rules to require cloture votes on all legislation.
(D) An increase in the number of senators led to longer floor debates and more procedural votes.

2. Which best describes the constitutional significance of this trend?

(A) The filibuster represents the Senate as a cooling saucer; its increased use shows the Senate functioning as the Framers intended.
(B) Increasing filibuster use reflects a departure from simple majority rule, raising questions about minority veto power in a democratic legislature.
(C) The data demonstrates the House has ceded most legislative authority to the Senate.
(D) Supreme Court decisions limiting executive power caused Congress to pass more legislation through procedural mechanisms.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → B
