Practice Questions

1. The exceptionally high third-party vote share in 1992 is most directly attributable to which of the following?

(A) A constitutional amendment temporarily eliminated winner-take-all Electoral College rules, making third-party candidacies more viable.
(B) Independent candidate Ross Perot ran a well-funded, nationally prominent campaign focused on deficit reduction, attracting nearly one-fifth of the popular vote.
(C) Voter dissatisfaction led to a sustained third-party movement that continued growing through the 1990s.
(D) The FEC temporarily relaxed campaign finance rules in 1992, enabling third-party candidates to raise more money.

2. The data best supports which argument about third parties in the U.S. electoral system?

(A) Third parties are gradually replacing the two major parties and will likely achieve major-party status within two decades.
(B) Third parties can achieve significant short-term vote shares under exceptional circumstances, but structural barriers including winner-take-all elections and ballot access laws prevent sustained electoral success.
(C) The Electoral College favors third-party candidates by allowing them to win individual states and deny either major party a majority.
(D) Third-party success correlates directly with economic conditions; they do well only during recessions.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → B
