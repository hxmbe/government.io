Practice Questions

1. Which best explains the sharp increase in public trust around 2001?

(A) A major economic expansion led citizens to credit the federal government with rising incomes.
(B) The September 11 attacks produced a rally-around-the-flag effect, temporarily uniting Americans behind federal institutions.
(C) A new president restored public confidence after years of political scandal.
(D) Congress passed sweeping domestic legislation that improved quality of life for most Americans.

2. The long-term downward trend from 1964 to 2024 most directly supports which conclusion?

(A) Americans increasingly prefer direct democracy over representative institutions.
(B) Third-party candidates have eroded public confidence in the two-party system.
(C) A sustained decline in political trust reflects growing disillusionment with federal institutions following major crises such as Vietnam, Watergate, and partisan gridlock.
(D) Federal programs have consistently failed their objectives, frustrating citizens.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → C
