Practice Questions

1. Which best explains the dramatic rise in the incarceration rate from 1980 to 2008?

(A) A constitutional amendment in 1984 expanded the list of offenses subject to mandatory minimum sentences.
(B) The War on Drugs policies beginning in the 1980s, combined with mandatory minimum sentencing laws, led to a rapid and sustained increase in the prison population.
(C) The Supreme Court ruled that stricter sentencing was required to protect Fifth Amendment due process rights.
(D) States increased police forces by 400%, leading to significantly higher arrest rates across all crime categories.

2. Critics who argue the trend raises Eighth Amendment concerns would most likely make which argument?

(A) Mass incarceration violates the Eighth Amendment prohibition on cruel and unusual punishment, particularly for nonviolent offenders subject to lengthy mandatory sentences.
(B) The Eighth Amendment guarantees the right to a speedy trial, and prison overcrowding reflects systemic due process violations.
(C) The Supreme Court should strike down all drug laws as violations of the right to privacy established in Griswold v. Connecticut.
(D) The Eighth Amendment bail clause is violated because wealthy defendants consistently pay lower bail than poor defendants.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → A
