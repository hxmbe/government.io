Practice Questions

1. Which best describes the trend in party identification from 1994 to 2024?

(A) Democratic identification has grown steadily while Republican identification has declined.
(B) Independent identification has grown significantly while both major parties have lost adherents, suggesting increasing disaffection with the two-party system.
(C) Republican identification has surged since 2016, making it the dominant party affiliation in the U.S.
(D) Party identification has remained essentially stable across all three categories.

2. The rise in Independent identification most directly relates to which challenge facing political parties?

(A) Declining voter registration requirements have made it easier to avoid identifying with a party.
(B) Third-party candidates have recruited large numbers of voters away from both major parties.
(C) Increased partisan polarization may drive moderate voters to reject major party labels even when their actual voting still aligns with one party.
(D) The Electoral College systematically disadvantages both major parties, encouraging independent identification.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → C
