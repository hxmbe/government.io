Practice Questions

1. The sharp spike around 2009–2010 is most directly explained by which of the following?

(A) A constitutional amendment expanded Congress power to fund state programs.
(B) Congress passed the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (2009) in response to the Great Recession, dramatically increasing aid to states.
(C) The Supreme Court ruled the federal government must provide block grants to all states.
(D) States negotiated new cooperative federalism compacts with the federal government.

2. The overall upward trend best illustrates which concept in American federalism?

(A) Dual federalism, where state and federal governments operate in entirely separate spheres.
(B) Devolution of federal authority back to the states, reducing the federal role in domestic policy.
(C) Cooperative federalism, where the federal government uses financial incentives to shape state policy priorities.
(D) Layer-cake federalism, in which state and federal governments share equal revenue.
Answer Key  ·  Q1 → B    Q2 → C
